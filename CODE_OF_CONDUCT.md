# Code of conduct

Be kind and be clear. Treat everyone here with respect, whatever their experience, background or the tools they use. Assume good intent, and say what you need plainly.

Not welcome: harassment, insults, personal attacks, sharing other people's private information, and spam or adverts in issues.

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, issues and listings that break these rules, and may block people who do not stop. To report a problem with behaviour, use the "Report content" link on GitHub or contact a maintainer through [a private security advisory](https://github.com/TheMightyTRex/stallboard/security/advisories/new) and say it is about conduct.
